## Brand & Style

This design system expresses Organic Modernism infused with Editorial Bio-Tech. It balances ecological stewardship with precision agronomy and culinary excellence, serving both B2B enterprise partners (culinary leaders, HoReCa, wholesale food purveyors) and health-conscious consumers.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Grounded:** Rooted in biological stewardship, land regeneration, and strict supply chain integrity.
- **Innovative & Progressive:** Highlighting scientific transparency, traceability metrics, and forward-thinking agrarian tech without sterile coldness.
- **Sensory & Natural:** Evoking pristine terroir, rich humus soil, fresh harvest, and culinary craftsmanship.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Structural Balance:** Magazine-grade typographic poise paired with clean biological data visualizations.
- **Quiet Depth:** Earthy tonal tiers rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows.
- **Tactile Refinement:** Subtle natural borders, warm alabaster canvas fills, and purposeful verdant accents that emphasize botanical vitality.

## Colors

The palette derives from the agrarian lifecycle: deep canopy foliage, fertile mineral soil, and vibrant nascent shoots, unified over a breathable, warm alabaster foundation.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Forest Green):** The commanding brand anchor. Used for primary navigation, critical calls-to-action, high-emphasis interactive states, and major section banners.
- **Secondary (`#8C533C` - Terracotta Soil):** Evokes earth, clay seedpots, and roasted ingredients. Used for secondary CTAs, B2B wholesale indicators, accent tags, and category distinctions. Supporting warm wheat (`#DDA15E`) serves as an ambient sub-accent for harvest highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#52B788` - Vibrant Sprout Green):** Represents active vitality, ecological compliance, live farm telemetry, and positive certification badges.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0F241A` - Deep Evergreen Slate):** Replaces pure black to ensure typographic depth with organic nuance. Applied across all body copy, dense data tables, and high-legibility headings.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: Soft Warm Alabaster White (`#F9F8F6`).
  - Card & Container Elevations: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Subtle Dividing Line: Warm Stone Grey (`#E7E4DC`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes three purposeful voices:
1. **Editorial Authority (`Newsreader`):** Used across display and headline scales. Conveys heritage, stewardship, and the prestigious pedigree of fine culinary provender.
2. **Humanist Ergonomics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers body and operational reading layers. Delivers high legibility across nutrition sheets, specifications, and consumer narratives.
3. **Bio-Tech Precision (`Space Grotesk`):** Drives labels, tracking numbers, lot IDs, traceability timestamps, metrics, and wholesale SKU tables.

### Rules & Formatting
- All uppercase labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must apply the specified tracking to ensure optical legibility.
- Italic variants of `Newsreader` are reserved exclusively for pull quotes, biological Latin classifications, or culinary notes.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid grid balances rhythmic whitespace with dense data capability:
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout, 64px (`margin-lg`) outer margin, 32px (`gutter-lg`) gutter. Max container width capped at 1360px for reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 24px (`margin`) outer margin, 24px (`gutter`) gutter.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`margin-sm`) outer margin, 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutter.

### Composition Guidelines
- Narrative sections (conservation initiatives, farm provenance) use open vertical spacing (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to preserve an editorial rhythm.
- Enterprise metrics and ingredient inventory utilize compact structural spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for scannable batch overviews.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, relying on tonal surface stepping and organic leaf-tinted boundaries.

### Tiers & Surface Stepping
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm Alabaster (`#F9F8F6`). Never shadowed.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border: `1px solid #E7E4DC`. Conveys crisp structure without visual weight.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Retains the `#FFFFFF` surface with an organic diffuse shadow tinted by the forest canopy: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Drawers & Traceability Modals):** Pure White with enhanced canopy diffuse: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 36, 26, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a 40% opacity neutral wash backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

A tailored soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`) reflects geometric restraint paired with natural softening:
- **Base elements (Inputs, Standard Badges, Buttons):** `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards, Panels, and Feature Displays (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Modals, Dialogs, and Sensory Imagery Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pill Exception:** Status indicators (e.g., "Certified Organic", "In Stock B2B") and interactive toggle pills adopt `rounded-full` (9999px) for clean data recognition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) background, Crisp White text, `Space Grotesk` medium font, 4px corner radius. Hover shifts to `#2D6A4F` with subtle 1px upward translateY.
- **Secondary / Terroir:** Terracotta (`#8C533C`) background, Crisp White text. Reserved for purchase orders, direct farm dispatch, and HoReCa sampling requests.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1px solid `#1B4332`, Deep Evergreen text. Hover fills with 6% opacity sprout tint (`rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.06)`).

### Badges & Chips
- **Bio-Conservation Badge:** Sprout tint background (`rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.15)`), `#1B4332` text, `label-sm` uppercase typography, pill-shaped.
- **Origin / Batch Chip:** Warm Alabaster background, 1px border `#E7E4DC`, `#0F241A` text. Includes an optional monospaced lot identifier prefix.

### Cards
- **Ingredient & Commodity Card:** Pure White surface, 1px solid `#E7E4DC` border, 8px border radius. Imagery carries a 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratio with natural contrast. Content area features editorial title (`Newsreader`), botanical subhead, origin coordinates, and real-time inventory metric.
- **Hover Behavior:** Border transitions to `#52B788` with Level 2 elevation shadow.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Fields:** Pure White background, 1px solid `#E7E4DC` border, 4px radius, 12px vertical padding. Focus state features a crisp 1.5px ring of `#1B4332` with no synthetic glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Forest Green fill when active with Crisp White glyphs; subtle 1px border in `#E7E4DC` when inactive.

### Lists & Partner Showcase Metrics
- **Data Lists:** Separated by hairline borders (`1px solid #E7E4DC`). Key data figures styled in `Space Grotesk` bold with small descriptive labels beneath.
- **B2B Metric Block:** Highlights soil organic matter %, carbon offset tonnage, or minimum order quantities (MOQ). Uses a soft muted background (`#F3F1EC`), large metric figures, and a Sprout Green indicator dot.
